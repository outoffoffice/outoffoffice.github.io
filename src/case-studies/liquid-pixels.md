Liquid art without paint, canvas or a studio to make a mess in: a digital take on the marble effect, built from nothing but Photoshop filters — wind, glass distortion, repeat. For Forward Festival's Moon Exhibition Lockdown Special, I turned the method into a step-by-step tutorial for creatives stuck at home.
