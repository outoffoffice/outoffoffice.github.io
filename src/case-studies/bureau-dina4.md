## A Playground

Bureau DINA4 was a place of my own to play visually — no client, no concept to defend, just a fixed format and whatever I felt like trying out that week.

The A4 sheet was the only rule. Every poster got a number and the same small label; everything in between was open — colour, glitches, type, texture, collage. Looking back, the series is less a finished body of work than a sketchbook: a record of what I was curious about at the time.
